Driftgate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Driftgate is a mobile and web game made by Kwadwo Baah in Ghana ("I", "me", "my"). This page explains what the game stores, why, and the choices you have. I keep it short because the game collects very little.

What the game stores

On your device. The game saves your best scores, the player name you choose, and your sound setting in the app's local storage. This stays on your device.

Online leaderboard. If the leaderboard is switched on, the game signs you in anonymously (no email, password, or account) and sends these items to the leaderboard database:

  • a random anonymous player ID created for your device;
  • the player name you type (up to 16 characters);
  • your best score for the day and the date.

Player names and scores appear publicly in the daily top ten, so please do not use your real name or any personal details as a player name.

What the game does not collect

Driftgate does not ask for or collect your name (beyond the player name you choose), email address, phone number, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or advertising ID. It does not use analytics or tracking tools.

Services that process data for the game

  • Supabase hosts the leaderboard database and the anonymous sign-in. Its privacy policy is at supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the game's fonts. When the game opens, your device asks Google's servers for the fonts, so Google receives your IP address and basic device information with that request. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

If you play offline, the game does not contact the leaderboard.

Ads and purchases

The game currently has no ads and no in-app purchases. If I add them, I will update this policy before the change is released and say what data the ad or payment provider collects.

Children

Driftgate is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given a player name or other information, email me and I will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Leaderboard entries are kept so that daily rankings work. To have your leaderboard entries deleted, email me at the address below with your player name and the day you played, and I will delete them, normally within 30 days. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes what is stored on your device, including your anonymous player ID. Entries already on the leaderboard remain until deleted on request.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. The leaderboard database uses row level security, so players cannot change each other's scores. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep what you share with the game to a minimum.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the data held about you, as provided by the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of Ghana and other laws that apply to you. Email me to do so.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
